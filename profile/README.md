# Everygen AI

## What will you make next?

A cover for a new idea. A photo with a little motion. Product footage for an upcoming campaign.

[Everygen AI](https://www.everygens.com/) brings those starting points together at [Everygens.com](https://www.everygens.com/). Choose an image or video workspace, develop your material, and take the result into your final edit.

## Pick a creative route

| You want to make… | Your starting place |
| --- | --- |
| A visual concept, cover, or still image | [AI Image Generator](https://www.everygens.com/ai-image-generator) |
| A moving scene from text or a supported reference | [AI Video Generator](https://www.everygens.com/ai-video-generator) |
| A short-form story or an animated photo | [Everygen Studio](https://www.everygens.com/everygen-studio) |
| Product material for an advertising edit | [Everygen Ads](https://www.everygens.com/everygen-ads) |
| Your own interpretation of a visual trend | [AI Effects Trend](https://www.everygens.com/ai-effects-trend) |

Studio and Ads provide individual clips and creative starting points. Finish the sequence, captions, and soundtrack in your editor. Model choices, supported inputs, and credit estimates are shown in the live workspaces.
